The Vanishing of Clara Hale — clickable prototypes (v4)

Two takes on the Detective’s Desk. Both share the same corkboard evidence board, the same case content (real script text), and the boxed-game flow: listen, read, look, answer Objective 1, then tear open Bonus Envelope A.

B · Clean Daylight Desk

Bright, minimal, top-down. Recordings play in a voice-memo app on a real-looking smartphone: list, play/scrub, line-by-line transcript, star lines. Document: the case file with flip pages.

D · Kitchen Table Investigator

Warm home setting. Recordings play on the cassette recorder with physical-style keys and a tape shelf. Document: the bakery ledger in an evidence bag, with a magnifier.
